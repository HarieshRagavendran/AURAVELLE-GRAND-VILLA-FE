Privacy Policy

At The Grand Auravelle Pool Villa, accessible from https://thegrandauravelle.com, the privacy of our esteemed guests is of paramount importance. This document details the types of personal information collected and recorded by our villa and how we utilize it.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a reservation enquiry through our website, we may request your full name, email address, telephone number, residential city, and travel dates. This data is collected solely to process reservation availability, communicate booking arrangements, and ensure personalized hospitality during your stay.

2. Data Protection & Security

We implement stringent administrative and technological security measures to protect your sensitive personal information against unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or interception. We strictly refrain from selling, renting, or trading guest details with commercial third-party marketing services.

3. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use standard session cookies and privacy-respecting analytics to gauge website responsiveness, device compatibility, and user navigational preferences.

4. Contact & Data Deletion Requests

To request inspection, alteration, or permanent deletion of your reservation contact records, please reach out directly to our villa administration at auravellegrand@gmail.com or via phone at +91 99446 12179.
